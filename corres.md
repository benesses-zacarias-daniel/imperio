## Colors
The palette is centered on a high-contrast dark theme. 
- **Primary Gold (#D4AF37):** Reserved exclusively for primary call-to-actions, active navigation states, and premium indicators.
- **Secondary Gold (#F1C75B):** Used for interactive states like hovers and micro-interactions to provide visual feedback without overwhelming the primary brand color.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background #D4AF37, Text #0E0E0E. Font-weight: 600. No border. On hover: Background #F1C75B.
- **Secondary:** Background: Transparent, Border: 1px solid #D4AF37, Text #D4AF37. On hover: Border #F1C75B, Text #F1C75B.

### Cards
- Background: #181818. Border: 1px solid #2A2A2A. 
- **Hover State:** Border color shifts to #D4AF37 (low opacity) and a subtle Y-axis lift of -4px using a CSS transition of 0.3s ease-out.

### Input Fields
- Background: #181818. Border: 1px solid #2A2A2A. Text: #FFFFFF. 
- Focus state: Border color #D4AF37. Placeholder text: #B8B8B8.

### Lists & Navigation
- Active state is indicated by a 2px bottom bar in #D4AF37 rather than a background change.

### Chips/Badges
- Used for "New In" or "Premium" tags. 
- Background: #2A2A2A, Text: #D4AF37
